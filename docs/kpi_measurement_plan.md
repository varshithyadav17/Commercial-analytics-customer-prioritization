# KPI & Measurement Plan — Commercial Prioritization

> All current records are synthetic. The measures validate the analytics workflow, not real commercial performance. Proposed criteria below are not achieved results.

## KPI catalogue
| KPI | Definition / grain | Current sample or status | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Account population | Distinct synthetic account IDs | 600 generated HCPs | Set planning population |
| Call event count | Number of synthetic contact-event rows | 8,036 generated calls | Validate activity population |
| Representative count | Distinct representative IDs | 40 generated reps | Establish assumed capacity |
| Assumed monthly capacity | Reps × 60 calls per rep / month | 2,400 calls / month under current assumption | Check plan arithmetic—not a validated staffing standard |
| Contact coverage attainment | Actual frequency / approved segment target and period | Pipeline computes target / actual comparisons | Identify coverage gaps |
| Planned activity vs capacity | Sum of planned calls vs assumed capacity at documented grain | Capacity assertion in pipeline | Check arithmetic / assumptions |
| Quota attainment | Simulated actual volume / simulated quota at rep-quarter grain | Generated output, illustrative only | Demonstrate transparent calculation |
| Plan override rate (future) | Overrides / planned assignments | Not currently measured | Assess practicality |
| Outcome change (future) | Change in an agreed outcome versus a credible comparison | Not measured | Evaluate only after real pilot |

## Metric contract to agree
Agree account identity; eligible activity types; what counts as a completed contact; reporting period; segment thresholds; handling of missing values; quota definition; fiscal quarter; and owner of capacity assumptions. Accounts, call events, account-month sales and rep-quarter quota have different grains.

## Proposed pilot acceptance criteria (not achieved)
1. Every reported KPI has a definition, grain, denominator, refresh period and owner.
2. Planned allocation reconciles to validated capacity and field constraints.
3. Segment and target rules are approved by the accountable business owner.
4. Use and override reasons are recorded during a shadow run / pilot.
5. Any claim of outcome improvement uses a pre-agreed comparator and controls for account mix, seasonality, territory and baseline trends.

## Evaluation design
1. Validate provenance, definitions, missingness and grain.
2. Shadow-run the plan without changing field activity.
3. Collect feasibility feedback and capture overrides.
4. Proceed to a limited pilot only after compliance, policy and data-governance review.
5. Measure coverage, feasibility, adoption and guardrails; evaluate outcomes with an appropriate design.
6. Continue, revise or stop based on evidence, not synthetic results.

## Guardrails
Field time and travel; contact policies and preferences; workload balance; unplanned cancellations; data quality and segment drift; explainability; and no causal interpretation of synthetic volume-per-call proxies.
